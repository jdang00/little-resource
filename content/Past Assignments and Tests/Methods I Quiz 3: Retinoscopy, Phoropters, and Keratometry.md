## Study Tips & Takeaways

- **Phoropter Components**:
  - Auxiliary lens wheel: Adjusts sphere power by $\pm 3.00\text{ D}$
  - "R" lens: $+1.50\text{ D}$ lens
- **Retinoscopy Technique**: Use sphere and cylinder dials to neutralize reflex
- **Final Prescription**: Sum of neutralization values minus working distance
- **Keratometry & Javal's Rule**:
  - Identify flat K and steep K readings
  - Use Grosvenor's Simplification for refractive astigmatism prediction
  - $\Delta K$ (change in keratometry) expressed as correcting cylinder
- **Vertex Distance**: Distance from spectacle lens back to eye front

---

## Important Formulas

- **Keratometry**: $\Delta K = K_{\text{steep}} - K_{\text{flat}}$
- **Javal's Rule**: Refractive astigmatism prediction from corneal measurements
- **Working Distance**: $P_{WD} = \frac{100}{d_{WD}}$ where $d_{WD}$ is working distance in cm
