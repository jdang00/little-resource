## Study Strategy

### Progressive Learning Path
1. **Master Quiz 1 concepts** - Build solid foundation in refractive errors
2. **Practice Quiz 2 techniques** - Develop retinoscopy skills  
3. **Integrate Quiz 3 methods** - Combine multiple measurement techniques

### Key Mathematical Relationships
- **Power Formula**: $P = \frac{100}{f}$ where $P$ is in diopters (D) and $f$ is in centimeters (cm)
- **Working Distance**: $P_{WD} = \frac{100}{d_{WD}}$ where $d_{WD}$ is working distance in cm
- **Keratometry**: $\Delta K = K_{\text{steep}} - K_{\text{flat}}$ for astigmatism prediction

### Clinical Skills Development
- **Terminology mastery**: Precise use of optometric vocabulary
- **Calculation proficiency**: Accurate mathematical computations
- **Instrument familiarity**: Understanding of clinical equipment
- **Problem-solving**: Application of concepts to clinical scenarios
