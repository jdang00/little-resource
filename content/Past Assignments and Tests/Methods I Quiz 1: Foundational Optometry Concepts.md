## Overview

This quiz covers foundational definitions and concepts of refractive errors and basic optics. It tests the ability to connect terms like myopia and hyperopia with their physiological causes and methods of correction.

## Key Topics

- **Myopia** (nearsightedness) and **Hyperopia** (farsightedness)
- **Astigmatism** and **Emmetropia**
- Lens correction principles
- Focal length and diopter relationships

## Study Tips & Takeaways

- **Know Your Definitions**: Understand the difference between myopia and hyperopia
  - **Hyperopia**: Light focuses behind the retina, corrected with plus (convex) lens
  - **Myopia**: Light focuses in front of the retina, corrected with minus (concave) lens
- **Focal Length Formula**: $P = \frac{100}{f}$ where $P$ is power in diopters and $f$ is focal length in cm
  - Example: $+2.50\text{ D}$ lens has focal length of $40\text{ cm}$
  - Myope with $20\text{ cm}$ far point needs $-5.00\text{ D}$ lens
- **Physiological Causes**:
  - **Myopia**: Eye too long or too strong for eyeball length
  - **Hyperopia**: Eye too short for optical surface power
- **Astigmatism**: Refractive error with toric optical surface

## Important Formulas

- **Power Formula**: $P = \frac{100}{f}$ where $P$ is in diopters (D) and $f$ is in centimeters (cm)
